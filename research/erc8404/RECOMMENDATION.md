# Recommendation

**COMPATIBLE_WITH_FROZEN_CORE — for the implemented external consumer route.**

The consumer reads the unchanged receipt and its exact profile/preimages, then
applies an independently supplied research contract to a separately named local
authorization decision. It does not enlarge the meaning of VERIFIED or
REPRODUCED inside an existing RVR, add receipt fields, or change old commitments.

This is not a blanket compatibility claim for arbitrary profiles, deployed
ERC-8274/WYRIWE/8412 integrations or a complete real execution chain. Those remain
unsupported or not implemented as stated in the matrix and limitations.

A first-class content-addressed RVR profile is a future additive option. Before
publishing it as conformant, specify its exact proposition/result, complete
dependency closure, schemas, byte contracts and every required conformance
control under new identities. Do not modify or reinterpret the existing Core.

Necessary conditions tested: required obligation coverage, exact preimage
commitments, authenticated subject/artifact/action/authority bindings,
scenario-dependent separation, and explicit authorization scope. Sufficiency is
conditional on trusted contract selection/adequacy, correct cryptographic and
Core verification, complete relevant evidence and the stated key-identity model.
These are experimentally tested conditional propositions, not formal theorems.

Proceed to independent technical review of the external consumer and contract
adequacy. Keep the publication as a Draft PR; no merge or upstream PR is part of
this experiment.
